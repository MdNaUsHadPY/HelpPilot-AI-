Return and Refund Policy

Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and returned with proof of purchase.

How to start a return
Contact our support team with your order number and the reason for your return. We will send you return instructions. Please do not send items back before you have received these instructions.

Refunds
Once we receive and inspect your return, we will email you to confirm the result. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5-7 business days. Original shipping fees are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect.

Return shipping costs
Customers pay for return shipping, unless the item arrived damaged, defective, or incorrect. In those cases we cover the return shipping cost.

Exchanges
If you would like a different size or colour, return the original item and place a new order. This is the fastest way to get the item you want, because stock can sell out.

Items that cannot be returned
Gift cards, personalised or custom-made items, and perishable goods cannot be returned. Items that show signs of use or wear are also not eligible for a refund.

Damaged or incorrect items
If your order arrives damaged or you receive the wrong item, contact us within 48 hours of delivery and include clear photos of the item and its packaging. We will arrange a replacement or a full refund.
