Frequently Asked Questions

Shipping

How long does delivery take?
Orders are processed within 1-2 business days. Standard delivery takes 3-7 business days after processing. Express delivery takes 1-3 business days after processing.

Do you offer free shipping?
Yes. Standard shipping is free on orders over $50. Orders below that amount have a flat shipping fee, which is shown at checkout before you pay.

Do you ship internationally?
We currently ship to domestic addresses only. International shipping is not available at this time.

How can I track my order?
Once your order ships, you will receive a confirmation email with a tracking link. Tracking information can take up to 24 hours to update after you receive the email.

Payments

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept major credit and debit cards and popular digital wallets. Payment is taken when you place your order.

Is my payment information secure?
Yes. Payments are handled by a secure third-party payment provider. We never store your full card details.

Can I cancel or change my order?
You can cancel or change an order within 12 hours of placing it, as long as it has not been shipped yet. After that time, please use our return process instead.

Contact and Support Hours

When is customer support available?
Our support team is available Monday to Friday, from 9:00 to 17:00 local business time. We are closed on weekends and public holidays.

How quickly will I get a reply?
We aim to reply to all messages within 1 business day.

How do I contact you?
Please use the contact form on our website, or reply directly to any of your order confirmation emails.
